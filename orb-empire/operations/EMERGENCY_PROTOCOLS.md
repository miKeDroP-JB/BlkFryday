# EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS
## Crisis Response Playbook

---

## SEVERITY LEVELS

| Level | Name | Description | Response Time |
|-------|------|-------------|---------------|
| GREEN | Normal | Normal operations | Standard |
| YELLOW | Developing | Situation developing | 1 hour |
| ORANGE | Concern | Active concern | 30 minutes |
| RED | Critical | Critical threat | Immediate |
| BLACK | Existential | Existential risk | DROP EVERYTHING |

---

## SCENARIO: IDENTITY INVESTIGATION

### YELLOW: Someone Asking Questions

**Trigger:** Pointed questions about who's behind the accounts

**Response:**
1. Do not engage with the questioner
2. Log the interaction (screenshot, link)
3. Monitor for 24 hours
4. Continue normal operations
5. Flag in next Athena briefing

**Messaging:** None. Silence is the response.

---

### ORANGE: Active Doxxing Attempt

**Trigger:** Someone publicly claiming to investigate, connecting dots

**Response:**
1. Alert JB immediately
2. Pause high-risk content (controversial takes)
3. Continue neutral content
4. Monitor all personas for related engagement
5. DO NOT confirm or deny anything

**Messaging if directly asked:**
"0RB speaks through the work. The rest is noise."

---

### RED: Partial Exposure

**Trigger:** Someone publicly claims to know identity with some accuracy

**Response:**
1. HALT all persona posting
2. JB assessment: How accurate? Source?
3. Evaluate: Deny? Ignore? Embrace?
4. If wrong: Complete silence (don't validate)
5. If right but unproven: Silence (force them to prove)
6. If proven: Execute reveal protocol

---

### BLACK: Full Exposure Against Will

**Trigger:** Identity definitively revealed with proof, gaining traction

**Response:**
1. HALT all operations
2. JB takes control of narrative within 4 hours
3. Options:
   - Own it: "Yes. One person built this. Here's what that means."
   - Reframe: Make story about mission, not person
   - Attack forward: "While you investigated, we shipped."
4. Rally community (they become defenders)

---

## SCENARIO: VIRAL MOMENT

### GREEN: Planned Viral

**Response:**
1. Athena flags in briefing
2. Prepare supporting content
3. Ensure product links work
4. Monitor for quality of new followers

---

### YELLOW: Unplanned Viral

**Response:**
1. Alert JB immediately
2. Assess: Is attention good or bad?
3. Pause scheduled content
4. Prepare to capitalize if positive

---

### ORANGE: Viral for Wrong Reasons

**Response:**
1. DO NOT respond immediately
2. Assess: Actually bad or just noisy?
3. If misinterpretation: One clear clarification, then silence
4. If legitimate criticism: Acknowledge without defensiveness
5. If bad faith attack: Complete silence

---

## SCENARIO: COMPETITIVE ATTACK

### YELLOW: Competitor Mentions/Copies

**Response:**
1. Screenshot and document
2. DO NOT engage publicly
3. Continue shipping (best response)

---

### ORANGE: Coordinated Attack

**Response:**
1. Alert JB
2. Assess: Organic or coordinated?
3. DO NOT engage in the fight
4. Let community defend
5. Continue shipping

---

### RED: Legal Threat from Competitor

**Response:**
1. HALT all related content
2. Consult legal immediately
3. Document everything
4. Do not discuss publicly

---

## SCENARIO: TECHNICAL CRISIS

### YELLOW: Tool Outage

**Response:**
1. Builder: "Aware. Fixing."
2. Fix the issue
3. Update when resolved: "Fixed."

---

### ORANGE: Major System Failure

**Response:**
1. Alert JB immediately
2. All personas pause product-related content
3. Hephaestus: "System maintenance. Back soon."
4. Fix the issue

---

### RED: Security Breach

**Response:**
1. HALT all operations
2. Contain the breach
3. Assess impact
4. If user data affected: Transparent disclosure required
5. Legal review

---

## SCENARIO: NEGATIVE PRESS

### YELLOW: Minor Negative Coverage

**Response:**
1. Do not engage
2. Document
3. Continue operations

---

### ORANGE: Major Negative Coverage

**Response:**
1. Alert JB
2. Assess: Fair criticism or hit piece?
3. If fair: Consider acknowledgment
4. If unfair: Document inaccuracies
5. DO NOT demand corrections publicly

---

### RED: Crisis-Level Coverage

**Response:**
1. All hands on deck
2. JB makes final calls
3. Prepare official statement via Ares
4. Legal review for defamation

---

## SCENARIO: COMMUNITY CRISIS

### YELLOW: Community Conflict

**Response:**
1. Hermes: "Keep it constructive."
2. Moderate as needed
3. Don't take sides publicly

---

### ORANGE: Bad Actor in Community

**Response:**
1. Document behavior
2. Remove/ban from community
3. Brief statement if needed
4. Don't name them

---

### RED: Community Turns

**Response:**
1. STOP and listen
2. Understand the grievance
3. If wrong: Acknowledge clearly
4. If misunderstanding: Clarify patiently
5. Demonstrate through action

---

## COMMUNICATION PRINCIPLES IN CRISIS

```
1. SPEED: Faster response (but not reckless)
2. CLARITY: Simple, direct language
3. HONESTY: Never lie, even when it hurts
4. ACCOUNTABILITY: Own mistakes clearly
5. ACTION: Show what you're doing
6. SILENCE: Often the best response to noise
```

---

## ESCALATION PATH

```
Normal -> Athena handles autonomously
    |
Yellow -> Athena flags in briefing
    |
Orange -> Athena alerts JB within 30 minutes
    |
Red -> Athena alerts JB immediately
    |
Black -> All operations halt, JB takes command
```

---

## POST-CRISIS REVIEW

After any Orange+ incident:

```
[ ] What happened?
[ ] How did we find out?
[ ] How did we respond?
[ ] What worked?
[ ] What didn't?
[ ] What do we change?
[ ] Update protocols?
```

## REMEMBER

**In crisis:**
- Silence is often strength
- Speed matters but not at expense of accuracy
- The community can be your defenders
- The work continues regardless
- This too shall pass

**The goal is never to "win" a crisis.**
**The goal is to emerge with trust intact.**
