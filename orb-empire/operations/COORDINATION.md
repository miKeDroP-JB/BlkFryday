## COORDINATION PATTERNS

### Pattern 1: Philosophy -> Product Flow

**Sequence:**
1. Apollo drops philosophical thread on [TOPIC]
2. 24-48 hours later: Hephaestus drops product that embodies it
3. 24 hours later: Hermes shows how to use it
4. Ares amplifies the connection

**Example:**
- Day 1, Apollo: "AI should augment humans, not replace them..."
- Day 2, Hephaestus: "Built a tool that augments your thinking. Free."
- Day 3, Hermes: "Here's how to get 10x value from the new tool..."
- Day 3, Ares: "This is what augmentation looks like."

---

### Pattern 2: Product-Led Flow

**Sequence:**
1. Hephaestus drops product with no preamble
2. Apollo provides philosophical context after
3. Hermes immediately supports with tutorials
4. Ares synthesizes

**Example:**
- Day 1, 9am, Hephaestus: "Voice Agent Builder. Free."
- Day 1, 3pm, Hermes: "How to set up your first voice agent..."
- Day 2, Apollo: "We're not building AI to replace your receptionist..."
- Day 2, Ares: "The Builder ships. The Prophet explains. The Coach enables."

---

### Pattern 3: Community-Led Flow

**Sequence:**
1. Hermes highlights community success/question
2. Other personas respond based on nature
3. Community drives content for 24-48 hours

---

### Pattern 4: Entity Revelation Flow

**Sequence:**
1. Personas operate "independently"
2. Speculation builds in audience
3. Ares reveals connection at strategic moment
4. All personas acknowledge

## TIMING RULES

### Same-Day Coordination

```yaml
allowed:
  - Hermes tutorial following Hephaestus drop
  - Ares amplification of any persona
  - Light engagement from all

not_allowed:
  - Apollo and Hephaestus both dropping major content
  - Multiple personas doing threads simultaneously
  - Heavy overlap that seems coordinated (before reveal)
```

### Weekly Rhythm

```
MONDAY
  Apollo: Philosophy thread
  Hephaestus: Product ship (if ready)
  Hermes: Week preview / strategy

TUESDAY
  Apollo: Light engagement
  Hephaestus: Benchmarks or feature spotlight
  Hermes: Tutorial content

WEDNESDAY
  Apollo: Industry commentary
  Hephaestus: Light engagement
  Hermes: Community Q&A

THURSDAY
  Apollo: Light engagement
  Hephaestus: Product ship or teaser
  Hermes: Strategy/business content

FRIDAY
  Apollo: Synthesis / reflection
  Hephaestus: Week roundup
  Hermes: Community spotlight

WEEKEND
  All: Light engagement only
  Ares: Amplification if needed
```

## CROSS-REFERENCE RULES

### Before Revelation

```yaml
allowed_references:
  - None. Zero direct references.

allowed_indirect:
  - Apollo: "Tools being built that prove this"
  - Hephaestus: "Philosophy behind the work"
  - Hermes: "There's more where this came from"

explicitly_forbidden:
  - Naming other personas
  - Tagging other accounts
  - "Check out @..."
  - Anything that confirms connection
```

### After Revelation

```yaml
allowed_references:
  - Full cross-referencing
  - Tagging each other
  - Explicit "0RB family" language

coordination_style:
  - Support without eclipsing
  - Amplify without redundancy
  - Each stays in their lane
```

---

## CONFLICT RESOLUTION

### Topic Overlap
1. Check: Whose lane is it primarily?
2. First mover gets the take
3. Second persona waits 24+ hours
4. Second perspective must add, not repeat

### Engagement Overlap
1. Most relevant persona responds
2. Other persona ignores
3. Never double-team (looks coordinated before reveal)

### Timing Conflict
1. Athena identifies in briefing
2. Highest-impact content gets prime slot
3. Other content reschedules

## COORDINATION CHECKLIST

Before any content posts:

```
[ ] Does this conflict with other persona's recent content?

[ ] Does this fit the current narrative arc?
[ ] Is timing optimal relative to other personas?
[ ] Does this maintain appropriate mystery (before reveal)?
[ ] Will this look coordinated when it shouldn't?
```
